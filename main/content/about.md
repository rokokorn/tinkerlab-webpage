Started in 2021, Tinkerlab began with ML projects for fast-growing startups and large global corporations. Over the past few years, we have worked with different companies on a diverse set of projects:

- Knowledge graph solutions with Neo4j for connecting fragmented business data and improving search, analytics, and decision support.
- LLM-powered assistants and internal AI tools for document understanding, workflow automation, and faster access to domain knowledge.
- Computer vision systems for quality inspection in manufacturing, helping teams detect defects earlier and reduce manual review.
- Applied ML projects delivered from prototype to production with a strong focus on practical deployment and measurable business value.

If you are looking for a fast solution that can be easily deployed to production at later stages, we are the right partner for you.
